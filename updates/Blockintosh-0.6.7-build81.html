Blockintosh 0.6.7 Beta · Build 81

0.6.7-beta build 81: Arke settings that stay how you left them, cleaner popups, fixed mod buttons, and smaller updates.

What's new since build 80?

• Arke now sets up its recommended graphics settings once, then leaves your choices alone. Turning something off or changing the upscaling no longer gets undone the next time you play.

• Fixed Arke setup enabling the separate spatial upscaler instead of DLSS via MetalFX. The old automatic preset gets corrected once, while settings youve already customized stay yours.

• Arke only shows its setup popup when something actually changed. It remembers your acknowledgment, and the popup now matches the rest of the launcher.

• Theres a one-time heads-up before changing graphics settings that could break Arke’s shaders. Cancel leaves your settings alone; continue saves your choice for that profile.

• Fixed the Install Mod button in LeviLamina’s Enhancements card. Both buttons fill the row properly, and stack on narrow cards so their labels dont get cut off.

smaller updates

• Sparkle can download just whats changed when updating from a recent release. It checks the update before installing and falls back to the full download when needed. New installs still get the full app. (introduced in 0.6.7-beta build 80)

requirements & beta notes

Requirements havent changed. Apple silicon and macOS 15+ are required for the launcher and FEX. GPTK 4 needs macOS 27+, GPTK 3 needs macOS 15.4+, and Mullion supports macOS 15+. Rosetta gameplay needs Rosetta installed. You need your own Minecraft for Windows license; Minecraft isnt included.

Still a beta, still not notarized. Mods, Arke and RTX presets depend on your Minecraft version and renderer. Back up important worlds before changing builds, runtimes or client mods.
