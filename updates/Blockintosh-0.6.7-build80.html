Blockintosh 0.6.7 Beta · Build 80

What's new since build 79.5?

• Fixed controllers not working on Rosetta. Blockintosh Wine 3.0 couldn't find its controller library there, so the game saw no controller at all. DualShock, DualSense and Xbox controllers work on Rosetta and FEX again.

• Quit anytime: press Cmd + Opt + Q, or hold D-Pad Up and Triangle (Y on Xbox) for two seconds. Minecraft closes right away, along with any Wine processes left behind, even if the game is stuck.

• Quitting this way keeps your settings safe. If Minecraft's options file gets cut short, it's restored from when you started playing.

• A small card slides in when the game starts with your gamertag and picture, and how to quit.

The launcher with a controller

• You can use the whole launcher with a controller. The D-pad or left stick moves between buttons, A presses, B goes back, LB and RB switch pages, and the right stick scrolls.

Arke & client mods

• Arke now sets itself up on every renderer: GPTK, Mullion and VKD3D-Proton, on both Rosetta and FEX. You'll see what it uses each time you play with Arke.

• With Arke, ray tracing is on and DLSS Enabled with MetalFX, with Metal 3 Fallback for Proper Rendering

• Enhancements are installed once per profile and shared by Rosetta and FEX. LeviLamina, your mods, BetterRTX and RTX tuning carry over between runtimes, and existing installs move over on their own.

Requirements & beta notes

Requirements haven't changed since build 79.5. Apple silicon and macOS 15+ are required for the launcher and FEX. Bundled GPTK 4 requires macOS 27+, GPTK 3 needs macOS 15.4+, and Mullion supports macOS 15+. Rosetta gameplay needs Rosetta installed. You need your own Minecraft for Windows license; Minecraft isn't included.

Still a beta, still not notarized. Mods, Arke and RTX presets depend on your Minecraft version and renderer. Back up important worlds before changing builds, runtimes or client mods.
